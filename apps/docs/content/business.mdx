# Business model

This page is the public summary of Konfide's business case. The canonical version lives in [`docs/BUSINESS_MODEL.md`](https://github.com/konfide-protocol/konfide/blob/main/docs/BUSINESS_MODEL.md).

## The market

Cross-border B2B payments globally is roughly $5T annually `[verify]`. The fastest-growing slice is intra-emerging-market trade — Africa to Asia, Latin America to Middle East. The Lagos–Guangzhou corridor alone is approximately $8B annually with median invoice size $5K–$50K `[verify]`. These are not the largest corridors in dollar terms; they are the corridors where the failure modes of traditional rails are most acute.

## The wedge

Lagos-Guangzhou first. Phone accessories importers from Computer Village specifically. Order frequency every two to four weeks, repeatable supplier pairs over years, invoice sizes in the right range for fees to bite, pain that is concrete and quantifiable. Founder is in Lagos.

## Customer profile

**Tunde** — 34, runs a phone accessories import business in Computer Village. Today: $400K/year in imports, 5-day Wise transfers, money-changer USDT-on-Tron after Wise compliance flags. Pays 4–7% all-in. With Konfide: 90-second settlement, ~80 bps cost, private, with a portable trust score.

**Wei** — 41, runs a phone-case manufacturing operation in Bao'an. Today: accepts Tron USDT because that is what his African customers send. Hates that competitors can read his pricing on the explorer. With Konfide: paid in USDC on Solana, amounts and counterparties private, audit path preserved for Chinese tax authority.

## Unit economics

A representative trade: $12,000 invoice, Lagos importer paying Shenzhen supplier.

| Component                 | Konfide take                |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Settlement fee            | 25 bps = $30                |
| FX spread share           | 30–80 bps = $36–96          |
| Float yield               | ~5 bps annualised (Phase 8) |
| Receivables financing     | 200–600 bps (Year 2)        |
| Premium tier              | $99–499/month (Year 2)      |

Per-trade gross take ~$80, blended ~67 bps. At scale ~80 bps once financing and premium tiers contribute.

For comparison: Tunde pays approximately 4–7% all-in today. Konfide compresses to ~80 bps. That is the wedge in one number.

## Moat

Four mostly independent dimensions, which is what makes the moat durable rather than just deep.

- **Trust-score data compounding.** Every settled invoice strengthens the model and increases the gap to a new entrant.
- **Corridor licensing burden.** Each new corridor requires regulatory work; one-corridor-at-a-time builds a moat per corridor.
- **Network density per corridor.** Once a corridor reaches ~100 active SME pairs, switching costs work in favour of the incumbent.
- **Privacy architecture expertise.** Selective-disclosure design is hard to get right and trivially easy to get wrong.
- **Founder market fit.** The first 200 customers come through the founder's relationships in Lagos.
