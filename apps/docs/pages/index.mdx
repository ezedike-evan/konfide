# Konfide

Konfide is a confidential B2B cross-border payment protocol built on Solana. Issuers send private invoices, payers settle in any chain or currency they prefer, and the protocol commits the result on-chain without leaking sensitive trade-finance data.

## Why

International B2B payments today are slow, expensive, and force counterparties to disclose more than they should. Konfide bundles cross-border rails (Kirapay), confidential settlement (Magicblock), cross-chain history (Covalent), retention primitives (Torque), and embedded wallets (Privy) into a single protocol that works for both sides of a trade.
